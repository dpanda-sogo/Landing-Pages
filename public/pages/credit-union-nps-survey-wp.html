The Solution

Four changes that moved the score

bolt

Event-triggered surveys

Surveys fire after a loan closes, an account opens, or a branch visit ends. Feedback arrives while the interaction is still fresh in the member's mind.

monitoring

Real-time NPS dashboards

Scores update as responses arrive. No waiting for a quarterly rollup to find out something changed.

campaign

Automatic detractor alerts

Low scores route to the right branch manager immediately, so the follow-up happens in hours, not weeks.
